Electric trains

Overhead wires and the trains that need them.

Electric locomotives are fast and cheap to run, but they only run under overhead wires.

  • Electric models: Aurora Electric, Swift High-Speed, Tempest Electric Freight.
  • Pick Electrify in Railways and drag along your track. Wires cost $220 a tile.
  • Every tile from the depot to each stop must be wired. The buy window tells you if a stretch is missing.
  • Bridges and tunnels count as wired.
  • Wires add $3 a tile to the monthly upkeep. Removing track takes its wires down too.
Tip The Zephyr Hydrogen (2022) needs no wires and is just as clean.

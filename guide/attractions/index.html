Attractions

Cinemas, resorts, funfairs and stadiums.

Bigger towns build attractions. Each draws many visitors, and passengers delivered to a station whose catchment covers one pay 30% more.

AttractionAppears in townsVisitors
Cinemaover 600 people60Evening crowds from all over town.
Seaside resortover 800 people, by the sea110Holidaymakers - busiest in summer.
Museumover 1,000 people45Visitors all year round.
Funfairover 1,600 people120Families - busiest in summer.
Stadiumover 2,200 people260Match day once a month: a crowd of fans to carry.
  • Visitors counts like people living there: an attraction makes as many passengers as a building of that size.
  • Funfairs and seaside resorts draw twice the crowds in June, July and August.
  • Match day: once a month, a stadium sends a crowd of fans to the stations that serve it. Two stations share the one crowd, as they share other passengers. The news tells you when.
Tip The town's window lists its landmarks and attractions. A station does not need to be next to one: its catchment only has to cover it.

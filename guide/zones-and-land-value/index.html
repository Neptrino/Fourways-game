Zones & land value

How good transport reshapes a town.

Every town balances homes, shops & offices and workshops. The three bars in the town's window show which it wants most.

  • Homes follow jobs. Good passenger service makes a town a better place to live.
  • Shops follow residents, and the goods and food you deliver.
  • Workshops follow residents and go where land is cheap.

Land value

Every plot has a land value from 0 to 100. Tall, dense buildings only go up where it is high, so a good station reshapes the town around it.

Landmarks

As a town grows it builds landmarks: church (over 700 people), park (over 1,300 people), town hall (over 2,400 people). Each raises the land value around it.

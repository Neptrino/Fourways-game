Every locomotive.

  • Each wagon holds 40 passengers, 40 newspapers, 20 electronics, 10 cars, or 30 units of anything else, and adds $6,500 to the price and $450 a year.
  • Steam engines lose 3% of their speed for every wagon they pull.
  • Electric models need overhead wires: Aurora Electric, Swift High-Speed, Tempest Electric Freight.
  • Any freight is every cargo except passengers, cars. Any bulk cargo also leaves out mail, newspapers.
  • A working life is 25 years for this kind: see Ageing & renewal.
Tip Steam tires. A steam engine loses 3% of its speed for every wagon it pulls, so long trains run better behind the Meridian Diesel (1960).

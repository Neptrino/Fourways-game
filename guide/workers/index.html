Workers

Bring commuters to an industry and it makes more.

Every industry employs people, and how much it makes depends on how many turn up. A bus or train full of commuters earns its fares and makes the mine or plant at the other end busier.

StaffOutput of a mine, farm or wellOutput of a plant, per unit delivered

Where the staff come from

  • Walking in: people from towns whose edge is within 8 tiles, more from big and close towns. They fill at most 55% of the jobs.
  • Commuters: passengers you deliver to a station whose catchment covers the industry. A stop there takes passengers even with no houses nearby. They pay the usual fare.
  • At the end of a shift about a third of the commuters want to go home again from the same stations.

  • A well-staffed industry is more likely to grow, and one short of staff to shrink.
  • A busy industry (at least 75% of its staff) is noisy: it lowers land values in a town up to 9 tiles away.
  • These need no staff: Power Station, Wind Farm Site, Wind Farm.
Tip The industry's window shows who works there, what it does to output and the nearest town to bring workers from.
